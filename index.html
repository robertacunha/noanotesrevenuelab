Noa Notes Revenue Lab

An acquisition system for Noa Notes, built around one number: quality sign ups at a CAC that scales. A sign up counts when the doctor records a first consultation. Owned channels come first, paid creative does the targeting, and every euro is measured against first consultations, continued use and the first paid month.

Approach

How the lab works

One person with a clear system and AI production can run the volume of a small team. The system decides what gets built, tested and kept.

01Tracking before spend

Pixels, Conversions API, UTMs and events into Amplitude and HubSpot tie every channel to the sign up, the first session and later the paying doctor.

02Read every stage every week

Each stage is read by channel and country, and the plays go where the numbers drop.

03Owned before paid

The doctors already on Docplanner marketplaces are the warmest and cheapest audience. Paid scales what the owned channels prove.

04Creative is the targeting

Broad audiences, many distinct concepts every week, produced with AI tools from idea to live ad in hours.

05One variable per round

Each test round changes one thing: the angle, the ask or the format.

06Winners become the standard

The winners of each round become the standard assets: the ad, the page and the nurture that carry the budget.

Layers

The four parts of acquisition

Owned databaseThe marketplace doctors

About 500K doctors, reached by email, in product and WhatsApp, segmented by specialty and country.

Paid ads and creativeCreative led acquisition

Meta, TikTok, Google Search, YouTube, Performance Max and LinkedIn, run with ecommerce discipline on CAC.

Pages and pitchPages that explain Noa

One page per specialty and country, with that specialty's template and a sample note, so a doctor knows what Noa does before signing up. A/B tested in each market's language.

Partners and new channelsNew channels early

Creators, newsletters, associations, congresses, and the answers AI assistants give doctors.
